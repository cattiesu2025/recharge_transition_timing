# One-dimensional work-to-return protocol (v0.6.0 masked-action pilot; not formally frozen)

This pilot isolates the decision of how much work to complete before returning. It does not test route planning. The environment extends MiniGrid 2.5.0 for rendering and Gymnasium compatibility, while the task state is a one-dimensional line `x=0..distance`. Work is available only at `x=0`; the terminal home dock is at `x=distance`. The displayed MiniGrid is 11 × 3 so all traversable cells lie on one row. One action is one time step.

The three atomic actions are `MOVE_LEFT=0`, `MOVE_RIGHT=1`, and `WORK=2`. There is no heading, turn, wait, charge, or return macro action. The policy controls every movement and may reverse after leaving work. Reaching the dock ends the episode, so there is no in-episode recharge. `quota` is the maximum available work, not a required amount; reaching zero remaining work does not terminate the episode.

Each observation contains normalized position, battery, remaining work, remaining time, scenario distance, and nonnegative dock margin, followed by the three valid-action bits. The environment masks `MOVE_LEFT` at `x=0`, masks `MOVE_RIGHT` at the dock, and masks `WORK` outside `x=0` or after the quota is exhausted. The mask never depends on reward condition, `safe_margin`, battery reserve, or predicted return. Both movement directions remain available inside the corridor. Masking is applied during replay-buffer warmup, epsilon exploration, greedy action selection, deterministic evaluation, and Double DQN next-action selection.

Movement costs one battery unit in either direction and WORK costs 3.5 units. `E_min_to_dock=(distance-x)×move_cost`, and margin is `battery−E_min_to_dock`. An action that costs at least the remaining battery ends in exhaustion; zero battery on the dock is exhaustion and takes precedence over arrival. Positive-battery arrival with at least one completed work unit is `returned`; positive-battery arrival with no work is `returned_without_work`. The 300-step limit is `time_limit` if no terminal outcome occurred.

The per-step pilot reward is `w_P × work_completed − w_R × clip((safe_margin − margin)/capacity, 0, 1) − time_cost`. Shared terminal terms add `return_bonus` for `returned`, subtract `empty_return_penalty` for `returned_without_work`, and subtract `exhaustion_penalty` for `exhausted`. RES/BAL/PROD differ only in `(w_P,w_R)`. No threshold or action mask forces a return. All reward values remain pilot candidates.

The primary ONSET candidate is an actual `MOVE_RIGHT` from `x=0` to `x=1`. It is confirmed by at least three actual moves toward the dock within eight actions including entry, before any leftward move, return to work, completed WORK, exhaustion, or episode end. The onset is recorded at the candidate entry step and confirmation at the third progress move. Dock arrival is not required. Failed candidates and rejection reasons remain stored. Auxiliary first-toward-dock, first-exit, dock, and sensitivity detectors with two and four progress moves are reported. A first exit followed by reversal is not an ONSET.

Development and held-out manifests each contain 27 matched scenarios. Development crosses distances 4/6/8, batteries 16/19/22, and work caps 6/8/10; held-out crosses distances 5/7/9, batteries 17/20/23, and caps 7/9/11. In each original scenario, at least one WORK followed by direct movement returns with at least `safe_margin`, while completing the full cap and retaining that margin is impossible. The sufficient-battery intervention sets initial battery to 60 with geometry and quota fixed; full work plus direct safe return is feasible. Training samples both regimes. Reference-controller probes establish feasibility only.

This pilot declares seeds 4100/4101/4102 and 600,000 actions per reward condition, using fixed-budget final checkpoints. Evaluation rows use schema 4 and diagnostics use schema 3. The action order, observation dimension, masks, and checkpoint output layer differ from v0.5.0; older checkpoints and trajectories cannot be loaded, decoded, or pooled under this protocol.

Diagnostics compare each learned trajectory with two preregistered computational references: the best successful direct route among `n=1..quota` WORK actions followed by only MOVE_RIGHT, and the exact finite-state optimum that also permits reversals. These references test reward and learning quality; they are not learned-policy evidence. Report completed work, confirmed ONSET, first exit, reversals, safe arrivals, empty returns, exhaustion, dock margin, mask violations, and regret. If ONSET and first exit coincide in all learned trajectories, report that the persistence confirmation did not alter event timing in this pilot.

R1 compares matched original scenarios with confirmed ONSET in both RES and PROD. Each seed contributes the median of `PROD onset − RES onset`; the candidate minimum remains 18/27 joint events per seed. Formal estimation requires a separate reviewed config, 20 declared seeds, immutable freeze, complete matched data, and held-out evaluation after freeze. Use a seed-median estimate, 10,000 seed bootstrap draws and an exact sign test. Missing ONSET is never assigned infinite time. Pilot, reference, smoke, and development results cannot serve as confirmatory R1 evidence.
